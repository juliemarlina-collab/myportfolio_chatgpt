02 / Design thinking

Smart DT

An exploration of design thinking as a practical way to frame learning challenges and make participation more purposeful.

Date / 2026My role / Learning experience designerRole / Learning designFocus / Design thinking in teaching and learning

Why this matters

Design thinking gives educators a structure for understanding a challenge, exploring ideas and testing a response. Smart DT sits in that part of my practice: connecting a method with activities people can actually use.

The work in progress

This portfolio entry introduces the direction of the project. The teaching context, materials, implementation record and feedback still need to be assembled into a complete case study.

What comes next

The case study will show the original learning need, the design process, examples of use and what changed after feedback. I will add those details when the supporting material is ready.

What I learned

A clear process helps learners see the reasons behind their decisions.

Evidence & next steps.

The project evidence and full process story are being prepared.
